deskforge / configurator

Build the desk.
Export one file.

Choose a theme and what sits on your Windows desktop: a clock, the weather, disk, memory and CPU, Claude status, the track playing, a dock and a Home launcher. Crypto prices are an optional widget. Leave them off and the panel never requests a price. The page writes desk.json. Hand it to your coding agent, or copy it to config\desk.json, and run the dry run. Nothing here touches your PC or calls any server.

Preview with made-up sample data. Fonts fall back to what your browser has.
